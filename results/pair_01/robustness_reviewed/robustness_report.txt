3. CORRECTED SENSITIVITY RANKING (BASED ON MAXIMUM TESTED DEGRADATION)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Based on empirical macro F1 degradation at the maximum tested stress conditions:

  RANK 1: REGISTRATION ERROR (Macro F1: 0.7359 -> 0.3028 at 50 cm / 2.0° combined stress)
          Rigid body misalignment shifts entire static surfaces across the decision boundary.
          (Note: Combined stress combines translation and rotation; isolated effects are detailed below).

  RANK 2: SPATIAL OCCLUSION (Macro F1: 0.7359 -> 0.5101 under Protocol A at 49.5% coverage loss)
          Unobserved blind spots in Scan 1 create phantom change clusters where static structures
          are falsely classified as removed due to missing measurement coverage.

  RANK 3: GAUSSIAN POSITIONAL NOISE (Macro F1: 0.7359 -> 0.6091 at sigma = 7.5 cm noise)
          High-frequency coordinate jitter scatters static points beyond 7.5 cm, degrading precision.

  RANK 4: POINT SPARSITY (Macro F1: 0.7359 -> 0.7166 at 10% point retention)
          Sparsity exhibits directional asymmetry: precision drops in Scan 0 due to wider point spacing,
          but Scan 1 remains remarkably stable when queried against a dense target.

9. EXPERIMENT E: SPATIAL OCCLUSION & DUAL EVALUATION PROTOCOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------
Cylindrical occlusion applied to Scan 1 with radius r in [0..7.6 m].

Two evaluation protocols are compared:
  - Protocol A (Strict False-Alarm Protocol): All Scan 0 points are evaluated against occluded Scan 1.
    Measures the detector's vulnerability to reporting false alarms when sensor coverage is missing.
  - Protocol B (Visibility-Aware Protocol): Scan 0 points inside the occluded cylinder are excluded.
    Evaluates change detection accuracy strictly within regions where both scans have valid visibility.

4.9       | 249,186     | 236,951      | 0.4646          | 0.5651        | 0.7180        | 0.5301          | 0.5966        | 0.7318

49.5      | 2,521,894   | 2,241,551    | 0.1803          | 0.2990        | 0.5101        | 0.4274          | 0.5358        | 0.6465       

Key Observations:
- In Protocol A, missing coverage creates severe phantom changes: Scan 0 precision drops to 18.0%
  and Macro F1 drops to 0.5101 at 49.5% occlusion.
- In Protocol B, when unobserved areas are acknowledged and excluded from verification,
  Scan 0 precision remains at 42.7% and Macro F1 stays at 0.6465 even when half the site is occluded.
- Scientific Conclusion: Unobserved regions must never be classified as physical geometric changes.
  A production construction monitoring system requires an explicit visibility mask or ray-casting
  audit before classifying geometric differences as actual progress or demolition.

10. OVERALL SYNTHESIS & MOTIVATION FOR STAGE 4
----------------------------------------------
This reviewed evaluation isolates each physical failure mode of standard Euclidean C2C:
  1. Rigid translation above 7.5 cm induces catastrophic false positives.
  2. Rigid rotation causes peripheral degradation proportional to radius.
  3. Occlusion generates severe phantom change alarms unless visibility-aware filtering is applied.
  4. High noise degrades precision along surface boundaries.

These empirical findings provide the benchmark against which M3C2 (Stage 4) and deep learning
change detectors (Stage 5) will be quantitatively measured.
