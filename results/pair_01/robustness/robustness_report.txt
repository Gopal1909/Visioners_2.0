Key Observations:
- Phantom Changes: Spatial occlusions create large 'holes' in Scan 1. Static points in Scan 0 that
  fall within these holes cannot find counterparts, producing large nearest-neighbor distances.
- This artificially inflates Scan 0 false positives (FP increases from 299,522 to 1,385,820 at 50% occlusion),
  dropping Scan 0 precision to 17.2% and Macro F1 from 0.7359 down to 0.5086.

7. OVERALL SYNTHESIS & MOTIVATION FOR STAGE 4 (M3C2 & LEARNING)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Ranking of failure severity for C2C on construction MLS scans:
  1. Registration Error : Most catastrophic (Macro F1 falls by 45.2% at 20 cm drift, and by 58.9% at 50 cm drift).
  2. Positional Noise   : High sensitivity above 2 cm (Macro F1 falls from 0.7359 to 0.6091 at 7.5 cm noise).
  3. Point Sparsity     : Directionally asymmetric (Scan 0 precision halved at 10% density).
  4. Spatial Occlusion  : Generates phantom change clusters in unobserved blind spots.

These empirical results rigorously establish why Euclidean C2C is inadequate for automated
construction monitoring and strongly motivate our upcoming stages:
  - M3C2 (Stage 4): Projects distance along local surface normals within cylindrical search
    neighborhoods and applies statistical confidence tests to filter high-frequency noise and tangential drift.
  - Deep Learning (Stage 5): Learns structural context to distinguish true physical construction
    changes from transient occlusions, density variations, and registration noise.
